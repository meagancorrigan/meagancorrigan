# FX Hedging

A model that compares three ways to hedge a foreign-currency receivable: a forward, a money-market hedge, and a put option. It shows what each one pays in dollars across a range of settlement rates.

Exercised on Scenario 20 (lithium-refining): a U.S. refiner owed EUR 11,000,000 by a German battery maker, due in 273 days.
